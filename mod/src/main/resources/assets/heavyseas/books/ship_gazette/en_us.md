# The Day's Run

Noon to noon:

- Lat. 41° 58' N.
- Long. 46° 12' W.
- Run, 531 miles.
- Wind light, N.W.
- Sea smooth.
- Barometer steady.
- Air 44°. Sea 38°.

# Sunday

Divine Service in the Dining Saloon at half-past ten. The Commander will read the prayers.

Hymn-singing in the Second Class Saloon after dinner. All are welcome.

# Notices

- The Library Steward lends books on signing the register. They are to be returned before the ship makes port.
- Deck chairs and rugs from the Deck Steward, four shillings each for the voyage.
- Beef tea on the Promenade Deck at eleven. Tea in the Lounge at four.
- Valuables may be left with the Purser.
- Wireless messages are taken at the Enquiry Office.
- Passengers are asked not to sit upon the rails.

# Concert

1. Selection: the Orchestra.
2. Song, "The Lamp in the Window": a Lady Passenger.
3. Recitation, "The Pilot's Daughter".
4. Violin Solo.
5. Song, "Westward Bound": by request.

A collection will be taken at the close.

God Save the King.

# Fog on the Banks

To-morrow night we cross the Banks of Newfoundland, where the warm water meets the cold. Fog is common there in April, and comes on quickly.

In fog the whistle is blown every two minutes, day and night. Passengers need not be alarmed by it.

The fishing schooners lie at anchor on the Banks, each ringing her bell. Those at the rail may see the first gulls. They do not go far from land.

# To-night

Clocks go back thirty-seven minutes at midnight, by the clock on the A Deck landing.

Sunrise at a quarter past five. The next number will be under your door by seven.

Good-night.
